Leak Detection & Repair in Falls Church, VA

Learn how visible and hidden plumbing leaks can be identified and addressed.

Signs of a hidden leak

Unexpected water usage, damp areas, staining, musty odors or unexplained moisture can indicate a leak. Not every sign proves a hidden leak, so proper diagnosis matters.

Detection process

Leak investigation may involve checking accessible plumbing, fixtures, pressure and visible signs of moisture. The exact method depends on the suspected location.

Protect your property

Address leaks promptly and monitor areas around fixtures, supply lines and exposed pipes for changes.

Why Choose a Professional Plumbing Service?

A professional assessment can help identify the actual cause of a plumbing problem instead of relying on guesswork. Service recommendations should be based on the condition of the plumbing and the needs of the property.
