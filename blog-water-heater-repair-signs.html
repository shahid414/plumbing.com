How to Know When Your Water Heater Needs Repair

Learn the common warning signs that a water heater may need attention.

What to Know

No hot water, inconsistent temperature, unusual noises and visible leaks are common signs that deserve assessment.

Common Warning Signs

Pay attention to changes in drainage, water pressure, moisture, fixture performance or unusual sounds. A single sign does not always identify the cause, so proper assessment is important.

When Professional Help Makes Sense

If a plumbing issue keeps returning, becomes difficult to control, affects multiple fixtures or risks property damage, consider contacting a qualified plumbing professional.

Next Steps

Write down what you are seeing, identify which fixtures are affected and share those details when requesting service.
